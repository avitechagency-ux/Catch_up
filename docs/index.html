Account Deletion Request Privacy Policy

Account Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your Cachap account and all associated personal data, you can request account deletion below or perform it directly inside the Cachap Android App under Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

What happens when your account is deleted?

  • Your account profile, login credentials, and session tokens are permanently removed.
  • For Students: Saved downloads, subject preferences, and teacher connections are erased.
  • For Teachers: Your uploaded notes, study materials, class connections, and unique teacher code are deactivated.
Your account deletion request has been received. Our team will verify and process your account removal within 24 to 48 hours.

Cachap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026


1. Introduction

Cachap is an educational platform built by Ssenyonga Moses to help teachers share learning materials digitally and help students access notes, revise lessons, and improve their academic experience.

2. Information We Collect

We collect account identification details (name, email, phone number, role), educational info (class, subjects, uploaded notes), and payment verification references via MarzPay for mobile money transactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information is used solely to authenticate users, organize educational resources, manage subscriptions, verify payments, and maintain service security.

4. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Users have full control over their personal data. You can delete your account permanently directly within the Cachap app via Profile Settings or by submitting the Account Deletion Request form above.
